    2025–26

    Purgo’s LangGraph agent turns data-engineering tickets into PySpark, SQL and dbt code.   • Holdbrief (opens in a new tab)

    2026

    A daily brief on the stocks you follow, for US and Indian markets, free during its beta. After each close it ranks every company against its market’s peers (301 US and 200 Indian stocks), values the price three ways around a fair zone that widens with uncertainty, and sets the stance by fixed, versioned rules. Every fact carries an ID, and a checker rejects any sentence of the explanation that cites nothing or a fact that isn’t there. Each view is logged and graded later against the index.
