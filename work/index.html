  • ReadableSTEM

    2026

    Turns math and science PDFs, and LaTeX source, into web pages a screen reader can read. Two vision models read each equation, every reading is checked against the PDF’s own text layer, and anything uncertain becomes a decision for the instructor. Live as a free beta that anyone can sign in to.

    2026

    Tests registered before any system ran. On 26 unseen documents it matched a Claude Opus chatbot on equation accuracy at about a third of the cost, with about half as many silent errors (wrong equations with no warning). The bars it didn’t reach are reported the same way.

  • Holdbrief

    2026

    A daily brief on the US and Indian stocks you follow. Every number and every Keep, Watch closely or Reconsider call comes from fixed, versioned rules in code. An LLM writes the explanation from those facts, and a checker removes any sentence it can’t trace back to them. Every published view is stored and graded later against the index.
