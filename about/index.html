About

I’m a software engineer at Purgo AI. I joined in June 2025 as an intern during my M.S. at UT Dallas and have been full-time since June 2026. Our agent turns data-engineering tickets into Databricks and dbt code, and I work on how it finds the tables a ticket needs and checks the code it writes. I also built our validation engine, which qualifies LLMs and Databricks platforms for regulated use.

On my own time I work on a11y-stem, which turns a STEM course PDF into an accessible web page. I score its equations against OpenStax textbooks, whose online editions include expert-written MathML.

Before Purgo, I interned at Digitus in 2024, where I built a text-to-SQL agent and an assistant over slide decks and videos, and at PwC in 2023, where I worked on sales forecasting. From 2019 to 2024 I was at IIT Madras, where I did a dual degree in biological sciences and data science and wrote my master’s thesis with The Jackson Laboratory.
