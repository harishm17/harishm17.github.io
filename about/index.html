I’m a software engineer at Purgo AI. I joined in June 2025 as an intern, on site that summer and then remote during my M.S. at UT Dallas, and have been full-time since June 2026. Our LangGraph agent turns data-engineering tickets into PySpark, SQL and dbt code. I work across it, from the prompts and model choices to how it finds the tables a ticket needs and checks the code it writes. I also built our two validation products, which qualify LLMs and Databricks platforms for regulated use.

On my own time I built ReadableSTEM, an open-source tool that turns a STEM course PDF into an accessible web page, now in a free beta that anyone can sign in to. I score it against OpenStax textbooks, whose online editions include expert-written MathML, and I published its held-out results, including where a general chatbot does better. I also built Margins, which turns a student’s own course files into cited notes and practice exams, and Holdbrief, a daily brief on the stocks you follow, where fixed methods set each stance and Claude writes the explanation, citing the facts behind every sentence.

Before Purgo, I interned at PwC in 2023, where I built a customer analytics platform, and at Digitus in 2024, where I built a text-to-SQL agent, a contract drafting tool and a helpdesk assistant that answers from documentation, videos and past tickets. From 2019 to 2024 I was at IIT Madras, where I did a dual degree in biological sciences and data science and wrote my master’s thesis with The Jackson Laboratory.
